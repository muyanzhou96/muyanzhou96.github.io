## Privacy Policy

This site is a personal academic homepage. It does not intentionally collect visitor information through forms, comments, or analytics in the current configuration.

If you contact me by email, your email address and message content will be visible to me through normal email delivery. I will not publish that information on this website without your consent.

### Log Files

The hosting provider may keep standard server or platform logs. Depending on the provider, these logs can include:

* Internet Protocol addresses (IP)
* Browser or device information
* Date and time of requests
* Referring pages
* Requested URLs

This site does not add custom tracking code to link that information to named visitors.

### Cookies and Web Beacons

The current site configuration does not use comments, advertising scripts, or custom analytics cookies.

If this configuration changes later, this page should be updated to describe the new services.
