I am a doctoral student in Software Engineering at Nanjing University. My research direction is software testing, with work on deep learning framework testing, model mutation, metamorphic testing, and testing techniques for large-scale model frameworks.

My recent work includes testing deep learning frameworks through developer expertise-based mutation, model-level metamorphic testing, model mutation, and empirical analysis of LLM-centric challenges for deep learning frameworks.
